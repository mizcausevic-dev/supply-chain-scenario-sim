# Kinetic Gain Embedded tie-back

This repo demonstrates the supply-chain scenario and recovery-packet control-plane shape of Kinetic Gain Embedded:

- disruption packets organized into one operator-readable lane for carrier, supplier, customs, demand, and inventory stress
- constraint and service-level drift routed without forcing teams into raw ERP, TMS, or spreadsheet workflows
- recovery packets that keep operations, finance, fulfillment, and customer-facing teams aligned on the same evidence

For embedded product work, the same primitive becomes an in-app supply-chain or logistics panel where operators need evidence-rich scenario routing without direct access to raw planning sheets, carrier consoles, or internal warehouse systems.
